Honesty note: “records no token” describes the snapshot's record — no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — not a guarantee that no token exists anywhere. Implied values are this radar's own estimates from the snapshot's figures, not predictions that a token will launch and not promises of any airdrop. Launch odds are a cross-sectional proxy: the share of similar protocols that already record a token. Protocols that have run for years without one may be structurally tokenless, so the top of the odds range deserves the most doubt.
